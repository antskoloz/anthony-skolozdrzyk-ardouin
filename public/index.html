About

Data-driven leadership, built across payments and finance

Revenue Operations & Data Analytics executive with 17+ years of international experience across PayPal, Nexi, Avis and Twenga. I help organizations transform complex data into growth strategies, scalable analytics capabilities, forecasting models and executive decision-making frameworks.

I'm a self-taught programmer covering the full data journey from raw logs to boardroom-ready insight — SQL, Python and Power BI — and I also build websites on the side (Laravel / VPS).

Multicultural and multilingual: born in France, based in Germany since 2016. I've also worked from Paris, Berlin and London, and lived 6 months in Mexico City.
